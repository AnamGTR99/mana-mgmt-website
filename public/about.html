About MANA.

MANA is an influencer and talent management agency in Melbourne, Australia, founded in 2025. We represent content creators Hugo Zbor, Kevin Chiang and Preston Young and run their brand partnerships from brief to final post.

How we work

A small roster

We take on a few creators at a time, so each one gets real management and every brand gets a fast answer.

One point of contact

Brands deal with MANA from the first email to the final post. Creators get to create.

Everything in writing

Deliverables, revisions, usage rights and dates are agreed before anything is filmed.

Built like a product

MANA is run by an engineer, Shei, who builds the agency's own tools so no brief, date or approval gets lost.
